# Phase 5AC — Pre-Outcome CC Execution Gate V1

## Status

**FROZEN_PRE_OUTCOME_CC_EXECUTION_GATE**

Phase 5AC binds the complete pre-outcome compute-fault analysis lineage before
any accepted prospective prediction or outer label payload is opened.

## Bound scientific lineage

The gate hash-binds:

- the frozen Phase-5Z prospective CC outcome-analysis protocol;
- the qualified Phase-5AA analysis core;
- the Phase-5AA qualification result and manifest;
- the qualified Phase-5AB artifact-I/O runner;
- the Phase-5AB qualification result and manifest;
- the frozen Phase-5E execution plan;
- the frozen Phase-3 timing manifest;
- the Phase-5O accepted-canary technical acceptance;
- the Phase-5U complete-estate technical acceptance.

## Future accepted inputs

The future accepted compute-FI estate is fixed to the already accepted
Phase-5 outer result root.

The outer dataset root is fixed to the frozen 300-ms / 50%-overlap dataset.

Phase 5AC records those paths as immutable future inputs but deliberately does
not stat, list, or open the accepted outer result root and does not load any
outer label payload.

Risk/timing information may be resolved only through the exact hash-bound
Phase-3 timing manifest.

No alternate timing source may be substituted.

## Scientific scope

Only `C0` and `CC` are in scope.

`CSC` remains excluded.

OnField remains excluded.

FP32 and PTQ remain separate.

Transient and persistent compute faults remain separate.

The three frozen operating points remain separate:

- `balanced`;
- `low_false_alarm`;
- `timely_150ms`.

All three checkpoint seeds remain retained.

No outer-test threshold retuning, checkpoint selection, fault resampling, or
outcome-driven protocol change is allowed.

## Required final executor behavior

A later final executor must bind the exact hashes of:

1. Phase-5Z protocol;
2. Phase-5AA analyzer;
3. Phase-5AB I/O runner.

It must verify artifact integrity before parsing prediction JSONL.

It must preserve:

- exact label joins;
- exact trial window counts;
- one fault scenario per `outer_instance_id`;
- persistent onset-to-trial-end integrity;
- validation-only frozen thresholds;
- historical event-decision semantics;
- paired clean counterfactuals;
- target/family strata;
- equal seed weighting within subject;
- subject-primary uncertainty.

Windows, repeated fault rows, and overlapping segments are not independent
inferential units.

Bad records may not be silently skipped.

## Authorization boundary

Phase 5AC does **not** authorize accepted prospective data access by itself.

It authorizes only:

- implementation of the final end-to-end outcome executor;
- synthetic/static qualification of that executor.

Before the first accepted prediction row or outer label array may be opened,
the following must occur:

1. implement the final end-to-end outcome executor;
2. qualify it without accepted prospective prediction or label payloads;
3. freeze a final one-way activation artifact binding that qualified executor
   hash.
